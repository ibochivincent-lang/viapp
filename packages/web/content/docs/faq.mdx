## Receiving a Gift

### Do I need a crypto wallet to receive a gift?
No. You simply open the link and sign in with Google, email, or a device passkey. ViApp automatically creates a non-custodial Stellar wallet for you in under 5 seconds. There are no seed phrases, no browser extensions, and zero initial crypto required. You can export your secret keys at any time.

### Who pays the network fee?
The recipient pays nothing ($0.00). ViApp's sponsor relayer submits the claim transaction to the Stellar Soroban network and pays all network fees via a Stellar fee-bump envelope.

### What if my friend never opens the gift?
The tokenized stock remains safely locked in the Soroban escrow contract until it reaches its expiration date (7, 30, or 90 days; default 30 days). Once expired, the sender can trigger `refund` at any time to return the assets to their wallet.

### What happens if I lose the link?
The private claim key is stored only inside the URL fragment (`#<key>`) in the link itself; it is never sent to ViApp's servers. If you lose the link, ask the sender to copy it from their sent history. If both parties lose it, the funds cannot be accessed until expiration, at which point the sender can reclaim them via `refund`.

### Can I turn my stock into cash?
Yes. Upon claiming, recipients have direct 1-click access to:
1. **Instant SDEX Liquidation:** Swap the tokenized shares for native Stellar USDC directly on the Stellar Decentralized Exchange.
2. **MoneyGram Cash-Out:** Withdraw physical cash at over 400,000 MoneyGram locations across 180+ countries.

## Sending a Gift

### Which stocks can I give?
You can give leading tokenized stocks including Apple (`AAPL`), NVIDIA (`NVDA`), Tesla (`TSLA`), Microsoft (`MSFT`), Amazon (`AMZN`), Alphabet (`GOOGL`), Meta (`META`), SPDR S&P 500 ETF (`SPY`), and Invesco QQQ Trust (`QQQ`).

### What payment assets are accepted?
Senders pay natively on Stellar using **USDC** or **XLM**.

### Is my gift note private?
Yes. Your personal note is encrypted in your browser using AES-GCM encryption with a symmetric key derived from the ephemeral claim secret. Only someone with the full link `#<key>` can decrypt and read the message.

## Technical & Custody

### How are the stocks backed?
Currently, listed stocks are backed 1:1 by institutional reserves via BNB Chain (`bStocks`), escrowed in the Soroban `viapp-gift-vault` contract (`CDEDHFJ7POEWDFFDRV74ZIWK4CSDKGEN4B4IQXUZHMKGVMJIVFODEV5M`). ViApp's active roadmap transitions to natively issued and directly liquidated tokenized securities on Stellar.

### Can ViApp take or divert my gift?
No. The Soroban smart contract enforces non-custodial custody. Only the holder of the ephemeral claim key signature can release a gift to their address. Neither the relayer nor the protocol admin can redirect assets to unauthorized recipients.
